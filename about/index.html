About us

Wiser Property Solutions protects and maintains homes nationwide: SFR, MFR, REO and occupied. We ensure every property is safe, compliant and market ready, with care you can count on.

We work for mortgage servicers, asset managers and real estate investors on residential properties across the United States, covering single family homes, multi family units, REO assets and tenant occupied residences.

Our work runs from inspections and preservation through to repairs, rehabs and seasonal maintenance, delivered in line with HUD guidelines and photographed so the condition of a property is always on record.

We are not a law firm, a mortgage servicer or a government agency. We maintain properties, and that is the whole of our interest in them.
