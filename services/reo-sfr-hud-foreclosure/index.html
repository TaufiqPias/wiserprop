Our services

REO / SFR / HUD / Foreclosure property solutions

We provide a full spectrum of services for REO (real estate owned), SFR (single family residential), HUD and foreclosed properties.

Our experienced team supports banks, investors and asset managers by handling everything from property upkeep and preservation to appraisals and eviction coordination. Each service is delivered in strict adherence to HUD regulations, ensuring full compliance and high standards.

We focus on streamlining the process and maximizing property value at every stage of the REO lifecycle.

What this covers

  • Initial secures and lock changes
  • Occupancy determination
  • Property upkeep and preservation
  • Appraisal and valuation access
  • Eviction coordination
  • Conveyance condition
